# Global AI Capital Terminal — Spec (user-supplied Oct 27, 2026)

Full spec as supplied in chat Oct 27, 2026, sections 1-13:
1. Core mission: global AI investment research terminal (banker + VC + equity research + IPO + strategist). Cover US, Canada, China, India, Europe, UK, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Middle East.
2. Mandatory Top 10s — separate, never combined:
   A. By cumulative fundraising (total raised, YTD, LTM, latest round/date, pre/post valuation, lead + participants, prior investors/stakes, primary vs secondary, # rounds, IPO status, growth, commercial developments, sources/confidence). Separate operating co from parent/subsidiary/project vehicles. No double counting.
   B. By latest disclosed valuation (private funding/tender vs public mkt cap/EV, methodology+source+date, revenue/growth/profit/CF, multiples, change vs prior, implied stakes, risks). Separate private and public tables, then labelled cross-market comparison. Never compare stale private vs current public without flagging dates/methods.
   C. Top 10 largest single rounds (amount announced vs actually contributed, type, leads, pre/post, equity/debt/convertible, primary/secondary, strategic conditions, vs previous round, implications).
   D. Top 10 investors by disclosed AI activity (by type: VC, growth, PE, SWF, pension, asset mgr/hedge, corporate strategic, family office, gov-backed). Trailing 12m and cumulative where measurable. Counts, companies, new/follow-on, exits, concentration. State when disclosure incomplete — never present partial data as total exposure.
3. Historical timelines per Top 10 company: every round inception→latest (date announce/close, round, amount, pre/post, investors, structure, dilution only if supportable, valuation change, revenue context, source/confidence). Cumulative by year, acceleration, time between rounds, valuation vs funding, investor composition change, commercial change.
4. Capital-flow patterns: VC↔labs, strategics↔model devs, SWF↔infra, semis↔compute, cloud↔models, PE↔mature software, public markets, secondary. For each pattern answer 10 questions (who, who receives, how much committed/announced/confirmed, structure, valuation, strategic rights, supplier/customer/lender overlap, reciprocal spend, vs investor's other AI bets, concentration risk). Flag circular financing with evidence; distinguish independent demand from investor-linked revenue.
5. Universe: foundation labs, semis/compute, cloud/infra, enterprise platforms/apps, robotics/physical AI, sector AI. Include emerging/regional, not only famous names.
6. Models & tech competition: per material release (name/dev/date/version, capabilities, independently verified benchmarks vs company claims, API/enterprise pricing, open/closed + licence, compute/training cost, adoption/customers/usage/revenue evidence, advantages/limits, competitive impact). Test whether tech progress translated to retention/revenue/margin/cost — never assume leadership = profitability.
7. IPO / pre-IPO / secondary / grey market: rumour verification status, filings, prospectus/underwriters/range/size/date, exchange/ticker/float/structure, primary vs secondary, pre-IPO rounds/valuations, tenders/employee liquidity, lock-ups, demand. Grey market: quote date/source/price/currency/share class, transaction vs indicative; implied valuation only if share count/terms support it. Always state grey quotes are unofficial, illiquid, may be inaccessible. Never fabricate quotes or claim a rumoured IPO filed.
8. Orders/contracts/customers: customer/supplier, dates, total value, firm vs optional/conditional, duration/milestones, annualised value, backlog/RPO, revenue recognition/cash, concentration/renewal, margin/capex. Distinguish signed PO from MoU/partnership/non-binding/potential. Contract value ≠ revenue ≠ cash ≠ profit.
9. Financials: public — filings (rev, growth, GP/margin, OI, EBITDA, NI, diluted EPS, OCF, FCF, capex, cash, debt, SBC, backlog/RPO/deferred, AI-specific rev/capex, guidance vs consensus/prior). Private — label source type (audited / mgmt / filing / journalism / estimate). Metrics EV/rev, EV/EBITDA, P/E, growth, burn, runway, capital intensity. Never apply public multiples mechanically to private; funding valuation ≠ fair value.
10. Dashboards: (1) Global fundraising, (2) Valuation evolution, (3) Investor activity, (4) IPO pipeline, (5) Commercial traction & financial quality.
11. Frequency: Daily change detection; Weekly Top 10/rankings/investors/valuations/IPO/contracts; Monthly comprehensive capital-flow + sector allocation + trends + valuation review; Quarterly earnings/revenue quality/capex/burn/ownership vs valuation support. Every report compares to previous observation; highlight adds/removes/rank changes/new valuations/stale data/corrections. No rank-change claim without equivalent datasets.
12. Evidence hierarchy: 1 filings/exchange/prospectus, 2 company IR/earnings/funding announcements, 3 gov procurement/court, 4 investor announcements/fund filings, 5 Reuters/Bloomberg/FT/WSJ, 6 VC/private-market DBs, 7 specialist tech/benchmarks, 8 private-market broker indications with limitations. Direct source links + publication date AND event date. Labels: CONFIRMED / REPORTED / INDICATIVE / RUMOUR / ANALYST ESTIMATE / UNKNOWN. Never invent. On disagreement present competing figures, explain date/method diff, identify best-supported.
    PLUS standing user rule (Oct 3, 2026): cross-check every figure against ≥2 independent sources before it goes out; if irreconcilable, label and hold.
13. Output structure (comprehensive reports, in order): 1 Exec summary, 2 Top10 cumulative fundraising, 3 Top10 valuation, 4 Top10 rounds, 5 Top10 investors, 6 Trends vs prior periods, 7 Institutional flows & strategic relationships, 8 IPO/secondary/grey tracker, 9 Contracts/orders, 10 Financials/multiples/cash, 11 Models & infrastructure, 12 Catalysts/missing info/data-quality warnings. Tables, historical charts where supported, source links, appendix. Per ranking: reporting date, currency, inclusion criteria, comprehensive vs disclosed-only.
