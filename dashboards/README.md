# Dashboards

1. **Global AI fundraising** — annual totals; by company, geography, stage, subsector; largest rounds by year; Top-10 share; YoY and TTM changes; equity vs debt, primary vs secondary; coverage limitations. Source: `data/fundraising_by_year.csv`, `data/ranking_a_*.csv`, `data/ranking_c_*.csv`
2. **AI valuation evolution** — latest private valuations + historical timelines; public market caps for listed peers; growth and dates; valuation/revenue multiples; valuation vs commercial progress. Source: `data/ranking_b_*.csv`, `companies/`
3. **Institutional investor activity** — most active disclosed investors; largest new + follow-on; concentration by company/subsector; cross-portfolio relationships; exits and secondary sales; participation change over time. Source: `data/ranking_d_investors.csv`
4. **AI IPO pipeline** — confirmed filings / announced plans / credibly reported candidates; valuations and offering details; expected dates; secondary transactions and verified unofficial pricing (labelled). Source: `data/ipo_pipeline.csv`
5. **Commercial traction & financial quality** — contracts and orders; revenue growth and margins; backlog and concentration; burn, FCF, capital requirements; revenue vs valuation; dependence on strategic investors/suppliers/customers. Source: `data/contracts.csv`, `companies/`
