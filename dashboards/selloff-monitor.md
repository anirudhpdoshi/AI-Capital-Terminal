# AI Selloff, Price Shock & Fundamental Risk Monitor

Ranked alert table (columns in data/shock_alerts.csv): Company | Ticker | Country |
Price change | Volume anomaly | Relative performance | Credit signal | Confirmed
catalyst | Financial risk | Contagion risk | Evidence links | Last updated.

Views: price-shock leaderboard; intraday price/volume charts; event timeline aligned
with price; peer + benchmark comparison; bond-vs-equity stress comparison; contagion
graph (from the Financial Network edge register — contractual vs inferred labelled);
capex/liquidity/debt dashboard; historical shock & recovery analysis
(data/shock_history.csv); source freshness + confidence on every row.

Severity: Critical is never assigned on price alone — it requires verified liquidity,
default, covenant or major contractual risk. A decline is never automatically
labelled a buying opportunity. Private-company watchlist events live in
data/private_watchlist_events.csv; no daily prices are shown for private names.
